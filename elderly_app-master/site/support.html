Common questions

My medicine reminders don't appear, or come late

Android: open Elderly Care, go to Medicines and, if you see "Reminders may arrive late", tap "Allow on-time reminders". Also check that notifications are allowed for Elderly Care in your phone's Settings, and that battery saving isn't restricting the app.

iPhone: open Settings → Notifications → Elderly Care and turn on Allow Notifications. If Focus or Do Not Disturb is on, allow Elderly Care to break through.

How do I move my information to a new phone?

Your information is kept only on your phone, encrypted, so it does not move by itself. On the old phone, open My profile → Back up my data and send the file to yourself (for example by email or WhatsApp). On the new phone, install Elderly Care, then open My profile → Restore from a backup and choose that file. You can protect the backup with a password.

I forgot my PIN

For your privacy the PIN cannot be recovered, not even by us. On the lock screen, tap "Forgot PIN?" to erase the app and start again. If you made a backup, you can restore it afterwards from My profile.

How do I change the language or text size?

Open My profile. Text size and Language are near the top.

Does Elderly Care see or store my information?

No. There is no account and no company server. Your information stays encrypted on your phone. The hospital search sends your location to OpenStreetMap, and video calls use Jitsi Meet, only when you use them. Read the privacy policy for the details.

How do I get a refund?

The app store you bought Elderly Care from handles payments and refunds. On iPhone, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play → your profile → Payments and subscriptions → Budget and history, or visit Google Play's refund page. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act are not affected.

Is it a medical app?

No. Elderly Care helps you remember and organise. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions. See the medical disclaimer.
