Try Elderly Care

This is the real app, running in your browser with sample data for Margaret, a 78-year-old with diabetes and high blood pressure.

  • Tap Medicines and answer a dose.
  • Open the Health tracker and add a blood pressure reading.
  • Tap I need help to see the emergency options. Nothing is really called or sent from the demo unless you choose to.
  • Choose Try it as a new user at the top to see the welcome screens and the step-by-step setup.

The demo forgets everything when you reload the page. The full app keeps your information, encrypted, on your phone, and adds phone reminders, Face ID or fingerprint unlock and the camera.
