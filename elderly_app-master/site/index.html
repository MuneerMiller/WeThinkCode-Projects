Medicines, appointments and help, made simple

Elderly Care is a calm, easy app for older people: medicine reminders, doctor visits, health readings and one big button to reach family or emergency services.

Everything stays on your own phone. No account, no tracking, no advertising, no subscription.

Medicine reminders

Reminders with Taken, In 10 minutes and Not yet buttons, a follow-up if a dose is missed, and a warning before your pills run out.

Doctor visits

Upcoming appointments, with a reminder an hour before.

Health readings

Blood pressure, blood sugar, weight and sleep, with charts and a summary to share with your doctor.

I need help

Call emergency services, text your family your location, or show your Medical ID, in one tap.

Easy to use

Large text and buttons, plain words, read aloud, and a short setup that makes the app yours.

Your language

English, Afrikaans, isiZulu and isiXhosa.

Elderly Care is a reminder and organiser, not a medical service. In an emergency, call 112 (from a cell phone) or 10177 for an ambulance.
