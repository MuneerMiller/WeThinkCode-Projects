# Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Elderly Care helps you look after your medicines, appointments and health. This policy explains, in plain words, what happens to your information.

## The short version

- Your information stays on your phone. There is no account and no company server.
- Your health information is encrypted on your phone.
- Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.
- There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data.

## What the app keeps

Only what you type in or add yourself:

- your name, contact details and medical details (such as age, blood group, allergies and conditions)
- your medicines, the times you take them, and whether you took each dose
- your appointments, notes, health readings and photos of documents
- the family members and contacts you add

## Where it is kept and how it is protected

- Everything is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
- Health information is encrypted (AES-256). The key is kept in your phone's secure storage and never leaves the phone.
- You can set a PIN, and on many phones use your fingerprint or face, so others cannot open the app.
- Your phone does not copy the app's data to the cloud automatically.

## When information leaves your phone

Only in these cases, and only when you use the feature:

- **Finding hospitals:** your current location is sent to OpenStreetMap's free Overpass service to look up hospitals near you. No name or other detail about you is sent.
- **Video calls:** calls use the free Jitsi Meet service (meet.jit.si). The room has a long random name and contains nothing about you. Your name is shown to the people in the call.
- **Calls, text messages and sharing:** these go through your phone's own apps (for example the Phone, Messages, WhatsApp or email app). What you send is handled by those apps and the people you send it to.
- **Backups:** a backup file is only made when you ask for one, and goes where you choose to save or send it. You can protect it with a password.

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, see or store your information on any server.
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
- We do not sell or share your information with anyone.

## Phone permissions

- **Location:** to find hospitals near you and, when you ask, to send your location to your family.
- **Notifications:** for medicine and appointment reminders.
- **Camera and photos:** to save photos of documents.
- **Fingerprint or face:** optional, to unlock the app.
- **Internet:** for the hospital search.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

## Your choices

- You can see and change everything in the app at any time.
- You can delete single items, or erase everything under My profile, "Erase all my data".
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.

Because your information never reaches us, we cannot see it, change it or recover it for you. Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and similar laws you have rights over your personal information, and with this app you can exercise them yourself, directly on your phone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be shown in the app with a new date.
